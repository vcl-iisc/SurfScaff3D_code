A four-step staircase. Each step is two flat quad patches: a horizontal tread and a vertical riser at the tread's front. The flight climbs along +X while rising in +Z.

**Coordinate system:**
- X is the direction of travel (each step advances 0.5)
- Z is height (each step rises 0.25)
- Y is the step width, constant 0..1 across the whole flight

**Construction:**
- Step i tread: a flat horizontal patch at height z_i = 0.25*i, spanning x_i..x_i+0.5
- Step i riser: a vertical patch at x = x_i+0.5, rising from z_i to z_i+0.25
- The riser's bottom edge equals the tread's front edge, and the next step's tread back edge equals the riser's top edge, so the whole staircase is one connected surface with no gaps
- All patches are flat 2x2 quads, so smoothness is high, s=0.97

    print(f"step{i+1} riser: x={x0+0.5:.3f}, z {z0:.3f}->{z0+0.25:.3f}, y 0->1")
